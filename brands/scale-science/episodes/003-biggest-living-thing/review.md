# B003 independent review

**Verdict: FAIL — 7/10.** Strong sourcing, clean caption numerals (caption_numbers.mjs: all 33 lines correct, no adjacent number words), all derived maths checks out (116.07/30 = 3.87; 6000/190 = 31.6; 965/42.6 = 22.7; 200/105.4 = 1.90; 42.6 ha / 0.714 ha = 59.7; 190/1.03 = 184 m³ → 1486.6/184 = 8.1; 180 km at 100 km/h = 1 h 48 min). It fails on one unsourced number, two unhedged superlatives and misleading map shapes. All are fixable in script/scenes.ts with no new assets.

## Issues

1. **Major, c3e2.** "At its widest, about 2 km across" and "around 25 minutes" on foot are on screen and in the narration, but neither has a row in sources.md and neither is in the fact pack (PLAYBOOK §7). The 2 km dashed line is also drawn across an equal-area Pando circle only about 0.74 km wide, so the line runs far outside the "Pando" shape. **Fix:** cut both lines and the path. If you keep them, add a sources.md row quoting the exact Pineau 2026 extent and the walking speed, and draw the line inside the real NAIP footprint rather than over the circle.
2. **Major, h2.** "the biggest animal that has ever lived" is unhedged. The fact pack (§3.1) says to avoid "largest animal ever" because fossil contenders are disputed. **Fix:** "the heaviest animal alive today" or "the biggest animal known to have ever lived".
3. **Major, c4i.** "the biggest one of all is hiding underwater" is an unqualified superlative. It contradicts c6b–c6d ("it depends on the ruler", and the Baltic *Fucus* spreads further). **Fix:** "And an even bigger one is hiding underwater."
4. **Major, RULER (h1, c5f, c5m2, c6f).** A straight, horizontal 180 km line labelled "this long (White Island → Faure Sill)" reads as the clone's real path and endpoints. The real span follows the bay, and neither endpoint is on that line. **Fix:** change the sub to "length only · not the real path". Alternatively, draw the ruler as a scale bar beside the bay rather than across it.
5. **Major, h4 / c5g / c5m / c5m2 / c4f.** The equal-area circles say "shape simplified", but the clone, Paris, Pando and fungus circles sit at made-up spots on real satellite imagery. In c5m2 the fungus sub has had its "position" note removed. The clone circle sitting over the real Shark Bay reads as the clone's real extent. **Fix:** set every sub to "same area · position & shape illustrative". In c4f use "Pando, for scale · not its real location".
6. **Major, promises t2/t3/th1.** The 180 km and twice-Paris promises are set at 0:00–0:35. Their payoff (c5e) lands at about 7:10 by word-count estimate, and the only seagrass beat in between is the c4i tease at about 6:17. That is a gap of about 6.5 minutes with no partial payoff. **Fix:** add one line in c4f: "And the Shark Bay plant? About twenty times bigger again." (200/9.65 = 20.7; add a derived row to sources.md.) Alternatively, add a seagrass callback in c3r. Re-check against the real durations after TTS.
7. **Major, c5h / c5l / c6 / c6e2 / c5k.** The *Posidonia oceanica* (Mediterranean) and Smithsonian diver photos run with no caption while the narration describes the Shark Bay clone ("It seems to set little or no seed…"). Viewers will read them as the clone. They are also CC BY images with no credit on frame (PLAYBOOK §7 asks for attribution in the frame). **Fix:** give each reuse a short caption: "Seagrass meadow (Mediterranean relative) · Karim saari, CC BY 4.0" or "· Symbiosis, CC BY 2.0".
8. **Minor, c5d.** "sampled ten of its meadows" assumes the answer, and then c5e says one meadow was a different plant. **Fix:** "sampled ten seagrass meadows across the bay".
9. **Minor, c4e.** The narration says "965 hectares" while the ring label that appears says "9.65 km²". Narration units should match on-screen units. **Fix:** ring label "Honey fungus · 965 ha (9.65 km²)".
10. **Minor, h5.** The chart is titled "by length" but includes Hyperion's height. The "???" bar is capped at 200 on a metres chart, which implies the answer is about 200 m, not 180 km. **Fix:** title "by length or height"; set the ??? note to "off this chart: kilometres".
11. **Minor, c5 (chapter card, narration, CLARITY).** "One Plant, 180 km" is close to the banned "180 km plant" wording. **Fix:** "One Plant, Spanning 180 km".
12. **Minor, c6e.** "more than 1,000 km² of seagrass disappeared" sounds like five times the clone's area. **Fix:** "more than a thousand square kilometres of seagrass, of all kinds, disappeared".
13. **Minor, c5e / c6c2.** There are 7 labelled pins for 9 clone meadows, and their positions are approximate (sources row 33), but the screen doesn't say so. **Fix:** add the sub "approx. positions · 7 of 9 meadows shown".
14. **Minor, c3b–c3r.** These scenes include `pando-z12` (EOX, CC BY) but credit only NAIP. The layer logic currently hides z12 because z16 covers every Pando camera, but that is fragile. **Fix:** drop `pando-z12` from the Pando scenes, or append the EOX credit.
15. **Minor, LICENSES.md.** The `bw-aerial` row (used in c1d) is tagged only "Scale & Science 001". OpenStreetMap (ODbL, which is not on the whitelist) is credited in package.json but has no row. **Fix:** tag bw-aerial for 003. Add an OSM row noting it supplied facts-only geocoding of 7 coordinates (an insubstantial extract), or replace those coordinates with values read off Edgeloe Fig. 1.
16. **Minor, c1e / c1h / c1i.** The Puerto Rico physonect photo appears while the narration describes the 47 m Ningaloo specimen. **Fix:** add to the caption "(a relative, not the Ningaloo specimen)".
17. **Minor, package.json hook / c5i.** The description's "a 47 m siphonophore" should read "a siphonophore whose outer ring was ~47 m (estimated)". In c5i, the Pando note "disputed" should read "evidence incomplete", which is the eLife wording.
