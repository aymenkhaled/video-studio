# Review round 1: Money & Maps 002 "Siberian Curse"

**Verdict: FAIL, 6.5/10.** The script and sourcing are strong: most numbers match sources.md, the hedges are kept, nothing from the "do not use" list appears, the balance line (c5g) and the not-advice credit are there, and the Gulag scenes are non-graphic. The failures are on screen: one invented number, clipped and overlapping text, pictures that contradict the narration, and too many dimmed text cards.

## Critical

2. **o6**: the title "THE SIBERIAN CURSE" is cut off at the left edge (it reads "HE SIBERIAN / CURSE"), and the caption covers the subtitle. Fix: scale the title to fit x 60–1860 and move the subtitle above the caption band.

## Important
