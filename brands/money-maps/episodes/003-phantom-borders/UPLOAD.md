Germany erased its East–West border in 1990, yet in 2025 not one of the five eastern states produced as much per person as the poorest western state. Poland's century-old partition lines, the Habsburg border, Rome's frontier and even a coastline from the age of dinosaurs still show up in today's money — and some don't. Which ghost borders fade, which ones stay, and why? Explained with maps, charts and real research. No background needed. Not financial advice.
