## Key facts (verify each in sources.md, URL + access date; hedge where marked)
- Born 24 Feb 1304, Tangier. Left 14 June 1325 at 21 for the Hajj, and did not return to Morocco for 24 years (Wikipedia, Britannica, The Conversation 2025-06-24).
- About 117,000 km (73,000 mi) in total, an **estimate** (Dunn). Marco Polo is about 24,000 km and Zheng He about 50,000 km (Wikipedia, citing sources). 117/24 ≈ 4.9, so say **"almost five times"**.
- Visited what is today 40–50+ countries (sources differ: say "around forty modern countries").
- He served about 8 years as a judge (qadi) in Delhi under Sultan Muhammad bin Tughluq, then as a judge in the Maldives.
- On the embassy to China he was shipwrecked and robbed (Calicut / Indian coast).
- He saw the Black Death in Damascus and Cairo in 1348, and learned his mother had died.
- He visited Mali and Timbuktu in 1352–53 under Mansa Sulayman.
- He dictated the *Rihla* to Ibn Juzayy in about 1355 on the order of Sultan Abu Inan of Fez.
- Europe barely knew the book until the 1800s. The full Arabic and French edition was by Defrémery and Sanguinetti (1853–58).
- **Hedge on screen:** historians doubt parts of the journey (some China and Bulgar passages may be borrowed), and the distances are reconstructions.
