## Brief corrections recorded 2026-11-18

1. **Delhi judgeship: brief said "about 8 years" → script says "about seven years"** (Wikipedia says six; Gibb and The Conversation say seven; we use the middle, sourced span and hedge). Rows 12–13.
2. **Departure age: brief said "21" → script says "about twenty-one", with the disagreement noted in sources.md** (Wikipedia/Gibb 21, The Conversation 22, Berkeley ORIAS 20). Row 2.
3. **"Visited 40–50+ countries" → script says "at least forty modern countries"** (ORIAS counts 44, The Conversation says more than 50). Row 6.
4. **Shipwreck and robbery separated**: the Calicut shipwreck and the later pirate attack are different episodes; the script does not merge them. Rows 15–16.
5. **Mother's death learned at home, not in Damascus or Cairo**: the brief attached it to the 1348 plague chapters, but ORIAS and Wikipedia place the news after the 1349 return to Morocco. Rows 20–22.
6. **Rihla date and order hedged**: completed about 1355 (possibly 1356); "at the suggestion/command of Sultan Abu Inan" rather than a bare "on the order". Row 24.
7. **1836 Cairo Rihla manuscript image DROPPED**: the Commons file page is CC BY-SA 4.0, which this studio rejects. Row 30.

## Map data

* Coastlines and modern borders: Natural Earth 1:50m/1:110m via `world-atlas` 2.0.2 (public domain data, ISC package).
* Route waypoints in `brands/history-maps/data/battuta.ts` are modern coordinates of the places named in the Rihla and in the sources above, joined in travel order (schematic, not a GPS track). The drawn line measures about 76,000 km great-circle.
* The on-screen HUD shows **"IBN BATTUTA (EST.)"**: the sourced ≈117,000 km estimate shared out along the drawn line in proportion to mapped distance, rounded to 1,000 km. It is an illustration of the estimate, not a measurement; a chip says so where it passes Polo (scene c3g).
* The grey Marco Polo line is schematic; the comparison bar uses the sourced ≈24,000 km figure.
* Doubted legs (Volga/Bulgar, China beyond Quanzhou) are drawn dashed and labelled.
